[manual_title]
Manual
---
[manual_body]
Decisions are saved on this device. Online sharing is not connected yet. Create a process with a clear question and collect proposals together.

Every new process includes Status quo and Repeat process. Score from -3 (strong resistance) to +3 (strong support); the dash means abstention. Negative scores are weighted.

Save each ballot before choosing Next participant. Each person has a separate local identity. Tap a saved participant to edit their ballot. Changing a name does not select another voter.

Results show ties and quorum. All ballots count by default; checkboxes let you explore a subset.
