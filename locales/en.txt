[manual_title]
Manual
---
[manual_body]
Uku helps groups choose options people can support and accept.

Create a process with a clear question, then share the link with participants.

During the proposal phase, people add options. Uku always includes Status quo and Repeat process so doing nothing or looking again are explicit choices.

During voting, each account scores every proposal. Negative scores are weighted so resistance is visible instead of hidden by unrelated support.
