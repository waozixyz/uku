Ukuvota Privacy

Ukuvota is designed as a local-first decision app. The website serves the app files and static project information.

The hosted web app may store process data in your browser storage so your local Ukuvota state can persist between visits. Clearing site data removes that local browser copy.

Ukuvota does not require accounts for voting. When you share a decision link or use a remote process, the information needed for that process may be sent to the service endpoint configured by the app.

Source code is available at github.com/waozixyz/uku.
